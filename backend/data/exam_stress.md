# Exam stress and academic pressure

Feeling anxious before quizzes, mid-sems and end-sems is extremely common, especially in a demanding environment like IIT. A bit of stress can sharpen focus; too much makes it hard to think, sleep or start studying at all.

Break preparation into small, specific blocks: "solve 5 problems from chapter 3" instead of "study thermodynamics". Small wins rebuild a sense of control, which is what stress takes away first.

Try 25-minute focused sessions followed by a 5-minute break away from the screen. After four rounds, take a longer break. Walk, drink water, look at something far away.

Sleep is not wasted study time. Memory consolidation happens during sleep, and an all-nighter usually costs more marks than it earns.

Comparing yourself with batchmates' CGPA or coding ranks fuels anxiety. Your worth is not your grade sheet. One bad exam is one data point, not a verdict on your future.

If exam anxiety is causing panic attacks, constant dread, or making you skip exams, talk to the institute counselling centre. Faculty advisors can also help if you need to discuss workload or a missed evaluation.
