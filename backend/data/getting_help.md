# Getting support on and off campus

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad has a student counselling service run through the Dean of Students' office. Check the official institute website or your student email for current counsellor names, timings and booking details. Sessions are free for students.

Talking to a counsellor does not mean something is seriously wrong. People go to sort out stress, relationships, family issues, academic setbacks, or just to think out loud with someone trained to listen.

What to expect in a first session: the counsellor will ask what brought you in, listen without judgement, and work with you on what you'd like to change. You decide how much to share.

National helplines (free):
- Tele-MANAS, Government of India mental health helpline, 24x7: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416
- iCall (Tata Institute of Social Sciences), counselling by phone, Mon-Sat 10am-8pm: 9152987821
- Emergency services: 112

If a friend is struggling: listen more than you advise, take any mention of suicide seriously, don't promise to keep it secret if their safety is at risk, and help them reach a counsellor or helpline. Look after yourself too.

If you are facing ragging, harassment or abuse, you can report it to the institute's anti-ragging committee or the national anti-ragging helpline (1800-180-5522). You deserve to be safe.
